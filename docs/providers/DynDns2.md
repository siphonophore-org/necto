# DynDNS v2

Open a record on the **DDNS** page, or select **Add record**, set **Provider** to **DynDNS v2**, then fill in:

- **Host name**: the fully qualified host.
- **Username**: your account username, or the update token, at the DynDNS v2 compatible service.
- **Password**: your account password or token.
- **Server override**: the update host. Left blank, members.dyndns.org is used. Set it to your service's update host.

_Ported from ddclient's `nic_dyndns2_update`._
