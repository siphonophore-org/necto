# ChangeIP

Open a record on the **DDNS** page, or select **Add record**, set **Provider** to **ChangeIP**, then fill in:

- **Host name**: the host to update, such as yourname.changeip.com.
- **Username**: your ChangeIP account username.
- **Password**: your ChangeIP account password.
- **Server override**: leave blank to use ChangeIP's own server.

_Ported from ddclient's `nic_changeip_update`._
